hecking the Temperature Rise Establishing the correct temperature rise and flow rate is very important in determining the proper operation of a combination boiler it is used many times during both manufacturers fault finding and XSol modes This short guide will show you a simple and quick way to carry out these checks How to do it &#10 &#10 1 Measure the temperature of the water from the cold tap allow the reading to stabilise then make a note of the result This will give you the reference point for the temperature rise &#10 &#10 2 Using the weir cup set the flow rate at the hot tap as per the manufacturers specification allow the appliance to run and the burner to ignite &#10 &#10 3 Place the temperature probe in the weir cup directly in the flow of the water monitor the temperature and allow it to stabilise Then as before make a note of the reading achieved &#10 &#10 4 Subtract the cold water temperature from the hot water temperature This figure is the Temperature rise Alternatively you may wish to use temperature clamps on the cold water inlet and hot water outlet pipes under the appliance but as it is not always possible to get access to these it is easier to use the method stated above Set the flow rate from the tap according to the information stated in the MI s Tools you will need A Weir Cup A thermometer or FGA + temperature probe @flowratesmall @fgaandtempprobe @arrowleftmed 2019 V3
