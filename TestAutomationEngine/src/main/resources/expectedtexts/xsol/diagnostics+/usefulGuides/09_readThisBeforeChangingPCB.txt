PCBs READ THIS FIRST 2017 V2 Before taking a PCB out of the box PCBs rarely fail they are usually misdiagnosed &#10 When PCBs do fail its normally a contact failure due to age an electrical surge such as lightning or a engineer building up a static charge for example by shuffling across the carpet before touching it A human can create up to 40 000 volts more than enough to fry an unsuspecting PCB To put this in perspective the shock you feel from a door handle is somewhere in the range of 10 000 volts The most common enemy of the PCB is something most of us use every day expanded polystyrene EPS Polystyrene such as packing material or a styrofoam cup can produce and store up to 10 000 volts Contact with these materials can easily build up a static charge Yes static electricity known as Electrostatic Discharge ESD will kill a PCB that s not an urban myth However ESD doesn t necessarily kill the PCB instantly it can actually cause damage that will lower the devices life span from the tens of thousands of hours to hundreds of hours How many times have you changed a 5 year old PCB only to have the new one fail in a month and its replacement fail within a year?
